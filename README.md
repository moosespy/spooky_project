# spooky_project
This is a Halloween platformer made for Terra Hack Club. It was made in pygame, to allow graphics.

You are the blue square with the black dot on it, and your goal is to touch the golden star at the top. There are obstacles in the way, though, and your run will end immediately if you touch one of them.

This is a top-down platformer, meaning that you jump and move left and right. This game also has scrolling features, so falling off the bottom of the screen will respawn you at the top of the screen, and moving off the right of the screen brings you to the left side of the screen.

To move, use the arrow keys or w/a/s/d. Press r to restart the game, but only once you've hit an obstacle or completed the run.

The obstacles are red spikes and black spears. The spikes exist in mid-air, and touching them makes you lose instantly. There are plenty of them, but they don't move. In contrast, the black spear (really just a triangle) flies from the right to the left, but there's only one of them. Similarly, touching it makes you lose instantly.

The platforms are interesting - you can go through the bottom of them, and you have to be careful about that, as holding the jump key while going through one could make you jump again, and you might not want that. Anyways, you can land on the top of them fine, and there is one platform that moves pretty fast, faster than the player can move, so you have to time it pretty well.

This game is demoed on codehs.com here: https://codehs.com/sandbox/trana0357/spooky-project/run. codehs requires you to hit the run button when you start, so hit the button, and the game graphics should load up. Once they do, you can start playing as I have shown!

This is my second pygame game, and I'm pretty proud of it! Thanks for checking out my project!
